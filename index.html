Tradition & Passion

A Decade of Celebrations in Pendurthi

Located conveniently opposite the Police Station on Sarada Nagar Main Road, Celebrations Restaurant (సెలెబ్రేషన్స్ రెస్టారెంట్) has evolved into Chinnamusidivada & Pendurthi's most celebrated dining address for families, friends, and food lovers.

Our culinary philosophy is rooted in authenticity: slow-marinated meats, hand-ground Andhra spices, fragrant long-grain basmati, and time-honoured dum cooking techniques. Every gravy is prepared fresh daily, without shortcuts, artificial enhancers, or compromises.

Whether you are marking a birthday, an anniversary, hosting a family get-together, or craving a midnight biryani craving with friends, our spacious air-conditioned dining halls and attentive staff ensure that every meal becomes a celebration.
